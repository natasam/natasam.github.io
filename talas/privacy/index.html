Talas privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Talas is published by Natasha Misic (natasam.github.io). Contact: greencodeinnovations@gmail.com.

The short version

Talas plays Balkan radio stations on your phone, in Android Auto and on Android Automotive OS. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and I don't receive any data about you. To find and play stations, Talas talks to a public radio directory and to the stations' own streaming servers, as described below.

What Talas sends, and to whom

  • radio-browser.info, a free, community-run directory of internet radio. Talas asks it for the stations of a country, for stations by id, and for the stations that match what you type in search. When you play a station, Talas also tells the directory that the station was played, which feeds the directory's public popularity count. These requests contain the search text or station id, not your name or any account.
  • The radio station you play. The audio comes directly from the station's streaming server, like in any radio app or web player.

Like any internet request, these servers see your device's IP address and a technical app identifier (user agent) and may keep them in their logs under their own policies. Talas doesn't send your location, contacts or any identifier of your device or account. Some stations only stream over plain HTTP, which is not encrypted; directory requests use HTTPS.

What stays on your device

  • Your favorite stations, recently played stations and settings such as the sleep timer.
  • They are stored only in the app. Removing a favorite deletes it, and deleting the app deletes everything.
  • Your device's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include this data if you have backup turned on.

Permissions

On Android, Talas uses internet access (to play radio), notifications (for the playback controls) and the foreground service permission that keeps playback running when the screen is off. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Sharing and reporting

If you tap Share, Talas opens your phone's share sheet with the station name and website; you choose where it goes. If you tap Report stream, Talas opens your email app with a pre-filled message to me containing the station's name and id. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

What Talas does not do

Talas has no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share personal data.

Children

Talas is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Radio stations broadcast their own content, which Talas doesn't control.

Your rights

Because Talas sends me no data, there is nothing on my side to access, correct or delete, unless you email me, in which case I keep the email only as long as needed to answer it. Everything the app stores is on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it. For questions or a request under the GDPR or another privacy law, write to greencodeinnovations@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes
