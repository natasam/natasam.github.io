Ingred privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Ingred is published by Natasha Misic (natasam.github.io). Contact: greencodeinnovations@gmail.com.

The short version

Ingred has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your history, favorites and meal log stay on your device. To look up a product, Ingred sends the barcode or the words you searched for to public food databases. If you choose to use an AI estimate, the meal photo you take (or the product details) is sent to Google's Gemini AI to produce the answer, and is not kept by Ingred.

What stays on your device

  • The products and additives you looked up, your favorites, your meal log, water and daily goals, and your settings.
  • They are stored only in the app, and deleting the app deletes them. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include them if you have backup turned on.

What leaves your device

  • Product lookups. When you scan a code or search, the barcode or search words are sent over an encrypted connection to Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts (run by the non-profit Open Food Facts association in France) and to USDA FoodData Central (U.S. Department of Agriculture), to find the product and its nutrition values. These requests contain no name, account or advertising identifier. Like any internet request, they show your device's IP address to those services, whose own privacy policies apply.
  • AI estimates (optional). Ingred asks before the first one. When you take a meal photo for an AI estimate, or open a product's AI insight, Ingred sends that photo, or the product's label data (name, brand, ingredients, nutrition values, additives) with Ingred's own additive verdicts and score, together with your answers to any follow-up questions about the meal, over an encrypted connection to Google's Gemini API through Google Firebase AI Logic, run by Google. Nothing else about you is sent: no name, no account, and not your profile, goals, life stage or spotlight choices. Google processes the request to return the answer. Ingred keeps the last AI insights on your phone so reopening a product doesn't send it again. Google's terms for the paid Gemini API say that prompts and answers are not used to improve Google's products. Ingred does not store your photos on any server. Firebase App Check sends Google a technical integrity token (from Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest on iPhone) to confirm the request comes from the genuine Ingred app. See Firebase privacy and security and the Gemini API terms.
  • Purchases. The first product checks are free; a one-time in-app purchase unlocks Ingred. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or Apple's App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. Ingred only asks the store app on your device whether your account owns the unlock. It never receives your payment details, name or email address.
  • Barcode scanner diagnostics (Android only). The Android scanner uses Google ML Kit, which sends Google limited technical data, such as device model, Android version, app version and performance metrics, to keep the scanner working. Camera images are not sent. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Camera

Ingred uses the camera to read barcodes and QR codes, and, only when you choose an AI estimate, to take a photo of a meal. Barcode images are processed on the device and are never saved or uploaded. A meal photo is sent for the AI estimate as described above and is not saved by Ingred.

Health Connect and Apple Health

If you choose to connect them, Ingred reads today's step count and active calories burned to show them next to your meal log. Ingred only reads this data and never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health. The data stays on your device: it is never sent to me or anyone else, never sold and never used for advertising. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect or in the Health app.

Ingred's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Ingred does not do

Ingred has no account or sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not sell personal data or share it for advertising. It does not collect your name, email address, location or contacts.

Children

Ingred is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because I do not receive or store personal data about you, there is nothing on my side to access, correct or delete. You can stop sending data for AI estimates at any time simply by not using them. Everything Ingred stores is on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it. For questions or a request under the GDPR or another privacy law, write to greencodeinnovations@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future feature sends more data, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.
