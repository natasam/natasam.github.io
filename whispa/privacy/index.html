Whispa privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Whispa is published by Natasha Misic (natasam.github.io). Contact: greencodeinnovations@gmail.com.

The short version

Whispa records and transcribes your voice entirely on your device. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it sends nothing to me or anyone else.

How it works

Whispa turns speech into text with the open-source whisper.cpp engine and speech models built into the app. Recording and transcription both run on your phone. On Android, Whispa does not have permission to use the internet at all. On iPhone, it makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

  • Your audio recordings, transcripts and note history, your personal dictionary, and your settings.
  • They are stored only in the app. Deleting a note deletes its recording, and deleting the app deletes everything.
  • On Android, recordings are excluded from cloud backup; your device's own backup may include transcripts and settings if you have backup turned on. On iPhone, your device's iCloud or computer backup may include Whispa's data.

Microphone

Whispa uses the microphone only while you record a note. While it records, Android shows a notification with a Stop button and iPhone shows the microphone indicator.

Purchases

Your first notes are free; a one-time in-app purchase unlocks Whispa. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or Apple's App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. Whispa only asks the store app on your device whether your account owns the unlock. It never receives your payment details, name or email address, and nothing about your notes is involved.

Copying and sharing

Text leaves Whispa only when you copy or share it yourself. It then goes wherever you paste or send it.

What Whispa does not do

Whispa has no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no cloud processing, and it does not sell or share any data.

Children

Whispa is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because Whispa sends me no data, there is nothing on my side to access, correct or delete. Everything Whispa stores is on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it. For questions or a request under the GDPR or another privacy law, write to greencodeinnovations@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
