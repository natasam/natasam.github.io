Weather Ahead privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Weather Ahead is published by Natasha Misic. Contact: greencodeinnovations@gmail.com.

The short version

Weather Ahead shows the weather along a driving route, at the time you'll be at each part of it. To do that it needs your trip's start and destination. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your saved places stay on your phone.

Location

Weather Ahead asks for your location to use it as the start of a trip. It reads your location only while you plan a trip in the app or use it in Android Auto, never in the background. You can say no and the app will tell you it needs a start point. You can change the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

What Weather Ahead sends, and to whom

  • Routing server (OSRM, an open-source route planner): the coordinates of your start and destination, to get the driving route.
  • Place search server (Photon, open-source search over OpenStreetMap): what you type when you search for a destination, and the coordinates of points along your route to find their place names. Your current position may be sent to rank nearby results first.
  • Weather server (Bright Sky, which serves the German Weather Service's open data): the coordinates of points along your route, about every 10 km, to get forecasts and official warnings.

These requests contain coordinates and search text, not your name, account or a device identifier, and they use encrypted connections (HTTPS). The servers see your device's IP address, as with any internet request, and may keep it in their logs for a short time for operation and security. The servers are run by me or by the services named above, only to answer these requests. Weather Ahead does not use your location for advertising and does not sell or share it.

What stays on your device

  • Your recent destinations, home and work places, and the trip you save for Android Auto.
  • They are stored only in the app. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Your device's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include this data if you have backup turned on.

Permissions

On Android, Weather Ahead uses internet access and location (precise or approximate, your choice). It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Sources and attribution

Weather data: Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD), via Bright Sky. Routes and places: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

What Weather Ahead does not do

Weather Ahead has no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share personal data.

Children

Weather Ahead is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Weather Ahead doesn't send me any data that identifies you, so there is usually nothing on my side to access, correct or delete. If you email me, I keep the email only as long as needed to answer it. For questions or a request under the GDPR or another privacy law, write to greencodeinnovations@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.
