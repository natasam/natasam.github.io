Magnet Meter privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Magnet Meter is published by Natasha Misic. Contact: greencodeinnovations@gmail.com.

The short version

Magnet Meter reads your phone's magnetic-field sensor and shows the result on your screen. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it sends nothing to me or anyone else.

How it works

The app reads the magnetometer (compass sensor) only while the Meter or Detector screen is open, or while you record a session. On Android, Magnet Meter does not have permission to use the internet at all. On iPhone, it makes no network requests.

Permissions

On Android the only permission is vibration, used for the detector's ticks. Reading the magnetic-field sensor needs no permission, and the app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

What stays on your device

  • The sessions you record (their curves and statistics) and your settings, such as the unit and detector sensitivity.
  • They are stored only in the app. Deleting a session removes it, and deleting the app deletes everything.
  • Your device's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include this data if you have backup turned on.

What Magnet Meter does not do

Magnet Meter has no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no cloud processing, and it does not sell or share any data.

Children

Magnet Meter is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because Magnet Meter sends me no data, there is nothing on my side to access, correct or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it. For questions or a request under the GDPR or another privacy law, write to greencodeinnovations@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
