Questions, problems or ideas: write to greencodeinnovations@gmail.com. I usually answer within two working days.

I bought the unlock. How do I get it on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Google account (Android) or Apple ID (iPhone), open the app, go to Settings and tap Unlock or restore, then Restore purchase. It's also restored automatically when the app starts.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play and the App Store. On Android, request a refund from your Google Play order history. On iPhone, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

All three apps keep your data on your phone only. Deleting a note, a favorite or a meal removes it; uninstalling the app removes everything. There is no account and nothing to delete on my side.

Magnet Meter readings look wrong or jumpy

Tap Calibrate on the Meter screen, hold the phone away from metal and move it slowly in a figure of eight for about ten seconds. Away from metal the reading should settle between 25 and 65 µT. Phone sensors react to iron, steel and magnets only, not to gold, silver, copper, aluminium or live wires.
